Fruit Slicer

Slice flying fruit with your hand in the air. Your camera is the controller.

  1. 1Stand your device up about an arm's length away, in a bright room.
  2. 2Swipe your hand through the fruit. Your fingertip sets off bombs, so keep it away from them.
  3. 3Pick Classic, Arcade (power-ups) or Zen (no bombs).

The camera picture stays on your device. The first start downloads the hand tracker (about 22 MB), then your browser keeps it.
