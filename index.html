Fruit Slicer

Slice flying fruit with your finger in the air. Your camera is the controller.

  1. 1Sit about an arm's length from the camera, in a bright room.
  2. 2Point your index finger and swipe fast through the fruit.
  3. 3Don't hit the bombs. In Classic, 3 misses ends the game.

The camera picture stays on your device. The first start downloads the hand tracker (about 22 MB), then your browser keeps it.
