# Fruit Slicer

A camera game: slice flying fruit with your finger in the air. Avoid the bombs.

**Play:** https://kolk42254-code.github.io/fruit-slicer/

## How to play

1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge (laptop or phone).
2. Tap **Turn on camera** and allow the camera.
3. Swipe your index finger through a fruit to choose a game.

- **Classic:** 3 missed fruit (or bombs) and the game ends.
- **60s Rush:** slice as much as you can in 60 seconds. A bomb takes 10 points.

No camera? Choose **Play with mouse or touch**.

## Privacy

The camera picture stays on your device. Hand tracking runs inside your browser with Google MediaPipe.
The first start downloads the hand tracker (about 22 MB); after that the browser keeps it.
